## Study Notes

Standard Real Book changes, as played by Chet Baker on *Chet Baker Sings* (1954).

Like Days of Wine and Roses, this is an **ABAC** tune: the A sections are identical, and B and C share their first two bars before splitting toward different endings. Two harmonic ideas carry the whole tune — a **minor ii–V to the relative minor** and the **IV → iv (backdoor)** move.

### A — tonic, then relative minor

**Bars 1–2 (EbMaj7):** **Eb major**. Target G and D; Ab is the avoid note.

**Bars 3–4 (Dm7b5 / G7(b9)):** Minor ii–V to Cm — borrowed from C harmonic minor. **D Locrian** on Dm7b5, then **G Mixolydian b9 b13** (5th mode of C harmonic minor). The `Ab` in both chords is the characteristic note — it contradicts the A natural of Eb major's IV.

**Bars 5–6 (Cm7):** vi of Eb, two bars. **C Dorian** or **C Aeolian** — both are diatonic to nearby keys; Aeolian keeps you in Eb major.

**Bars 7–8 (Bbm7 / Eb7):** ii–V to **AbMaj7** (IV). **Bb Dorian** / **Eb Mixolydian**: the `Db` is the new note.

### B — IV, backdoor, and V/V

**Bar 9 (AbMaj7):** IV — **Ab Lydian** (D natural) keeps a link to Eb major.

**Bar 10 (Abm7 Db7):** **Minor-plagal / backdoor**: iv–bVII7 resolving to EbMaj7. **Ab Dorian** → **Db Mixolydian** (or Db Lydian dominant, with G natural). Guide tones: `C → Cb → Bb` on the 3rds.

**Bars 11–12 (EbMaj7 / Cm7):** I–vi.

**Bars 13–14 (F7):** II7 — V/V for two bars. **F Mixolydian** (A natural is the new note). F Lydian dominant (B natural) adds shimmer.

**Bars 15–16 (Fm7 / Bb7):** ii–V back to Eb. **Eb major** over both.

### C — the ending

**Bars 25–26:** Same as the B section (IV → backdoor).

**Bars 27–28 (Gm7 / C7):** iii → V/ii — a secondary dominant pulling to Fm7. **G Phrygian** (from Eb), then **C Mixolydian b9 b13**.

**Bars 29–32 (Fm7 / Bb7 / EbMaj7 / Fm7 Bb7):** Final ii–V–I plus turnaround.

### Practice strategy

1. **Compare B and C:** play bars 9–16 and then 25–32 back to back. Same start, different destination (II7 vs. iii–VI7).
2. **Hear the Ab:** loop bars 1–5 and make the `Ab` (b9 of G7) resolve to `G` (5 of Cm7).
3. **Backdoor guide tones:** on bar 10, play `C → Cb` (3rd of Abmaj → Abm) then `Cb → Bb`.
4. **Chet's approach:** sing the melody first. His solos stay close to it and use mostly chord tones. It's a good model for lyrical playing.
