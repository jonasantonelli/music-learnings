## Study Notes

Chart follows the standard Real Book changes, as played by Miles Davis on *Walkin'* (1954).

Solar is a 12-bar tune — but not a blues. It's a chain of ii–Vs that **tonicize a new key every two to four bars**, each one a whole step lower than the last: `C minor → F major → Eb major → Db major → back to C minor`.

**Bars 1–2 (CmMaj7):** Tonic minor with a major 7 — use **C melodic minor**. The B natural is the color tone; let it ring.

**Bars 3–4 (Gm7 / C7):** ii–V into F. The minor tonic *becomes* a dominant: `Cm → C7` just raises the 3rd (`Eb → E`). Use **G Dorian → C Mixolydian**, or think one scale (F major) across both bars.

**Bars 5–6 (FMaj7):** First new key center. **F major** (or F Lydian).

**Bars 7–8 (Fm7 / Bb7):** Same trick as bars 3–4, a step lower: the tonic turns minor (`A → Ab`) and becomes the ii of Eb. **Eb major** over both bars.

**Bar 9 (EbMaj7):** Second key center, one bar only. **Eb major**.

**Bar 10 (Ebm7 Ab7):** Once more: Eb goes minor and becomes ii of Db. **Db major** over the bar.

**Bar 11 (DbMaj7):** Third key center — Db is the bII of C, so this is also a Neapolitan color. **Db major** or Db Lydian.

**Bar 12 (Dm7b5 G7(b9)):** Minor ii–V home to C minor. **D Locrian → G altered** (or C harmonic minor across the bar).

### Guide tones

The 3rds and 7ths move by half step through the whole tune — the chain `Bb → A → Ab → G → Gb → F` (7ths of each ii and 3rds of each V) is the backbone of any solo.

### Practice strategy

1. **Arpeggiate only the maj7 targets:** `CmMaj7 — FMaj7 — EbMaj7 — DbMaj7` to hear the whole-step descent.
2. **Hear the major → minor pivot:** play `FMaj7 → Fm7` and `EbMaj7 → Ebm7`, lowering just the 3rd. That single-note move is the whole trick of the tune.
3. **Play it in all keys:** at 12 bars it's the most efficient ii–V drill in the Real Book.
