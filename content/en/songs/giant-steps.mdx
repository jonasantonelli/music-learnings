## Study Notes

Chord changes follow Coltrane's original recording on *Giant Steps* (1960) — the standard Real Book chart.

### The three tonics

Everything orbits three major keys a **major third apart**: `B → G → Eb` (an augmented triad). Each is reached by its own V7 (or ii–V), so the tune modulates every one or two beats. This is the full cycle that the bridge of *Have You Met Miss Jones* only hints at.

| Tonic | V7 | ii–V |
|---|---|---|
| BMaj7 | F#7 | C#m7 – F#7 |
| GMaj7 | D7 | Am7 – D7 |
| EbMaj7 | Bb7 | Fm7 – Bb7 |

### Bars 1–8 — descending cycle

**Bars 1–3 (BMaj7 D7 | GMaj7 Bb7 | EbMaj7):** Tonic → V of the next tonic, twice. Each key lasts two beats. Play **B major → D Mixolydian → G major → Bb Mixolydian → Eb major**. The simplest line is the **1-2-3-5 pattern** on each chord (Coltrane's own device): B C# D# F# | D E F# A | ...

**Bar 4 (Am7 D7):** ii–V back into G — the first full ii–V. **A Dorian / D Mixolydian** (one scale: G major).

**Bars 5–7 (GMaj7 Bb7 | EbMaj7 F#7 | BMaj7):** Same descending cycle one step later: `G → Eb → B`.

**Bar 8 (Fm7 Bb7):** ii–V into Eb for the second half. **Eb major** across the bar.

### Bars 9–16 — ii–V to each tonic

The second half slows the harmonic rhythm: each tonic gets a full bar followed by a ii–V to the next one — `Eb → G → B → Eb`, moving **up** by major thirds.

**Bars 9–10 (EbMaj7 | Am7 D7):** Eb major, then G major for the ii–V.

**Bars 11–12 (GMaj7 | C#m7 F#7):** G major, then B major for the ii–V.

**Bars 13–14 (BMaj7 | Fm7 Bb7):** B major, then Eb major.

**Bars 15–16 (EbMaj7 | C#m7 F#7):** Eb major, then the ii–V turnaround back to BMaj7 at the top.

### Guide tones

The 3rds and 7ths move by half step or common tone through the whole cycle — e.g. `D# (3 of B) → C (7 of D7) → B (3 of G) → Ab (7 of Bb7) → G (3 of Eb)`. Singing this line is the fastest way to hear the changes.

### Practice strategy

1. **Tonics only:** arpeggiate `BMaj7 GMaj7 EbMaj7` slowly until the major-third jumps feel natural.
2. **1-2-3-5 on every chord:** at ♩ = 80, then push the metronome up in small steps.
3. **Guide-tone line:** one note per chord (3rd or 7th), whole chorus, no mistakes before adding rhythm.
4. **Play Countdown next:** same cycle applied to the Tune Up changes.
