## Study Notes

Common lead-sheet changes in D minor, based on Jobim's own reading of the tune (*The Composer of Desafinado, Plays*, 1963). Jobim's harmony varies from chorus to chorus; compare with the recording and adjust the second half to taste.

### A — chromatic descending bass

**Bars 1–4 (Dm6 → C#dim7 → Cm6 → G7/B):** The heart of the tune is the bass line `D → C# → C → B`, one half step per bar. Each chord is a re-harmonization of that falling line:
- **Dm6** — tonic. **D Dorian** (the B natural is the 6th).
- **C#dim7** — works as a rootless A7(b9) (V of D minor). **C# whole-half diminished**.
- **Cm6** — passing chord on the line. Its notes (C Eb G A) are the same as Am7b5 or a rootless F9. **C Dorian** (or C melodic minor).
- **G7/B** — V of C with the 3rd in the bass. **G Mixolydian** (b9 optional).

**Bar 5 (BbMaj7):** bVI of D minor. **Bb Lydian**.

**Bar 6 (Em7b5 A7(b9)):** Minor ii–V back to D. **E Locrian** then **D harmonic minor from A** (A Phrygian dominant).

**Bars 7–8 (Dm6):** Tonic. The second eight bars repeat the first, ending on **D7** (V of Gm) to set up the B section.

### B — relative major and back

**Bars 17–20 (Gm7 C7 FMaj7 BbMaj7):** A ii–V–I in **F major** (the relative major), then IV of F. **G Dorian → C Mixolydian → F major → Bb Lydian** (all F major, except the E natural over Bb).

**Bars 21–24 (Em7b5 A7(b9) Dm6 D7):** Back to D minor via its ii–V, then D7 again to repeat the relative-major phrase.

**Bars 29–32:** Same ii–V–i, with **A7(b9)** in bar 32 as the turnaround to the top.

### Practice strategy

1. **Bass line first:** play just `D C# C B Bb A D` as whole notes with the melody. The whole A section is voice leading.
2. **Inner voice:** in a Dm6 voicing, keep the top voices still and move only the bottom note down by half steps — this produces all four opening chords.
3. **Two key centers:** mark the B section's F major phrase and practice switching between **D minor** (harmonic/melodic minor on the V) and **F major** material.
