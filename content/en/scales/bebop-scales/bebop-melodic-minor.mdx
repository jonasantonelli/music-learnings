The **bebop melodic minor** scale is jazz melodic minor with a chromatic **♯5**
added between the 5th and the 6th — the same passing tone placement as
[bebop major](/lessons/scales/bebop-scales/bebop-major), but over a minor tonic.
It targets a **minor 6** (or minor-major 7) chord.

### Interval formula

| Degree | 1 | 2 | ♭3 | 4 | 5 | ♯5 | 6 | 7 |
|--------|---|---|----|---|---|----|---|---|
| Semitones from root | 0 | 2 | 3 | 5 | 7 | **8** | 9 | 11 |

The bold ♯5 is the passing tone. The other seven notes are the jazz melodic minor
scale (1 2 ♭3 4 5 6 7) — see the [Melodic Minor](/lessons/scales/jazz-scales/melodic-minor)
page for that parent sound.

### What it's played over

Use it over a **tonic minor** chord — `m6` or `m(maj7)`. Over `Cm6` or
`Cm(maj7)`, play **C bebop melodic minor**. The chord tones it targets are those
of the minor 6: **R, ♭3, 5, 6**.

### The rhythmic rule

Run it in eighth notes from a chord tone on beat 1 and the m6 chord tones land on
the downbeats. Descending **C bebop melodic minor** over `Cm6` from the root:

| Beat | 1 | & | 2 | & | 3 | & | 4 | & |
|------|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Note | C | B | A | G♯ | G | F | E♭ | D |
| Role | **R** | 7 | **6** | *♯5* | **5** | 4 | **♭3** | 2 |

The chord tones **C, A, G, E♭** (R, 6, 5, ♭3) all fall on the beat, with the
chromatic G♯ passing through on the upbeat — structurally identical to bebop
major, just with a ♭3.

### Practice tips

- **Vamp on `Cm6` and descend from the root** in eighths until the alignment is
  automatic.
- **Stack it against bebop major** in the same key — only the 3rd differs, so
  it's an efficient pair to learn together.
- **Use it for tonic minor, not the ii chord** — for a `ii-7` reach for
  [bebop dorian](/lessons/scales/bebop-scales/bebop-dorian) instead.
- **Treat the ♯5 as pure passing color**, never a resting note.
