The **bebop dorian** scale (also called the **bebop minor** scale) is Dorian with
a chromatic **natural 7th** wedged between the ♭7 and the root — the same passing-
tone trick as the [bebop dominant](/lessons/scales/bebop-scales/bebop-dominant),
applied to a minor tonic. It's the minor-chord member of the bebop family, used
over **minor 7th** chords.

### Interval formula

| Degree | 1 | 2 | ♭3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | ♭7 | 7 |
|--------|---|---|----|---|---|---|----|---|
| Semitones from root | 0 | 2 | 3 | 5 | 7 | 9 | 10 | **11** |

The bold ♮7 is the passing tone; the other seven notes are plain Dorian
(1 2 ♭3 4 5 6 ♭7).

### What it's played over

Use it over a **minor 7th** chord with a Dorian color — a `ii-7`, or any vamping
`m7`. Over `Dm7`, play **D bebop dorian**. The chord tones it targets are those of
the minor 7th: **R, ♭3, 5, ♭7**. (The natural 6 keeps it Dorian rather than
Aeolian, so it also sits well over `m6` and `m13` voicings.)

### The rhythmic rule

The added ♮7 turns the seven-note Dorian into an **eight-note** scale, so an
unbroken eighth-note run starting from a chord tone on beat 1 lands the m7 chord
tones on the downbeats. Descending **D bebop dorian** over `Dm7` from the root:

| Beat | 1 | & | 2 | & | 3 | & | 4 | & |
|------|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Note | D | C♯ | C | B | A | G | F | E |
| Role | **R** | 7 | **♭7** | 6 | **5** | 4 | **♭3** | 2 |

The chord tones **D, C, A, F** (R, ♭7, 5, ♭3) all fall on the beat, with the
chromatic C♯ passing through on the upbeat — structurally identical to bebop
dominant, just over a minor tonic.

### Practice tips

- **Vamp on `Dm7` and descend from the root** in eighths until the chord-tones-on-
  the-downbeats alignment is automatic.
- **Pair it with bebop dominant** — the passing-tone placement (between ♭7 and
  root) is identical, so the two scales share a feel even though one is major and
  one minor.
- **Keep the ♮7 chromatic** — it's a passing tone, not a leading tone to rest on.
- **Start the line from any chord tone** (R, ♭3, 5, ♭7) on a downbeat to preserve
  the alignment, not just from the root.
