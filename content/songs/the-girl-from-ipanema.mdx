## Study Notes

Standard Real Book changes in F, as played on *Getz/Gilberto* (1964). The recording's vocal choruses are in Db; the chart stays in F, the usual jam-session key.

### A sections — two bars each of I, II7, ii, bII7

**Bars 1–2 (FMaj7):** Tonic. **F major** (or F Lydian — the B natural fits Jobim's melody color). Target the 3rd (A) and 7th (E).

**Bars 3–4 (G7):** **II7**, the secondary dominant V/V, but it doesn't resolve to C. It moves to Gm7 instead, so the chord works as a bright, non-functional color. **G Lydian dominant** (G A B C# D E F) matches the melody's #11 (C#). Guide tones: `E → B` (7 of FMaj7 to 3 of G7).

**Bar 5 (Gm7):** ii of F. **G Dorian**. The 3rd drops `B → Bb`, a half-step guide-tone move.

**Bar 6 (Gb7):** **Tritone substitute for C7**. Resolves down a half step to FMaj7. **Gb Lydian dominant** (same notes as C altered). Guide tones across Gm7 → Gb7 → FMaj7: `F → Fb(E) → E` and `Bb → Bb → A`. The 7th of Gm7 slides into the 7th of Gb7, which is already the 7th of FMaj7.

**Bar 8 (Gb7):** Turnaround back to the top via the tritone sub. In the second A this bar stays on FMaj7 and leads to the bridge.

### Bridge — distant keys, each reached by a chromatic slide

The 16-bar bridge is built from three pairs of chords, each dropping by a **minor third** or tritone, before a normal ii–V–I setup back to F.

**Bars 17–20 (GbMaj7 → B7):** GbMaj7 is a half step above the tonic, a sudden **Neapolitan** shift. Play **Gb major**. B7 is V of E, but instead of resolving there it becomes part of the next ii–V: think `F#m7 B7` spread over four bars. **B Mixolydian**.

**Bars 21–24 (F#m7 → D7):** F#m7 continues the B7 color (**F# Dorian**, same notes as B Mixolydian). D7 is V of G and sets up Gm7. **D Mixolydian**, or **D half-whole diminished** for tension.

**Bars 25–28 (Gm7 → Eb7):** **G Dorian**, then Eb7 (**Eb Lydian dominant**). Eb7 is bVII7 of F, but here it's heard as a pivot into the Am7 that follows.

**Bars 29–32 (Am7 D7(b9) / Gm7 C7(b9)):** Two back-to-back ii–Vs: first to Gm (`Am7 D7(b9)` as ii–V of ii), then home (`Gm7 C7(b9)`). **A Phrygian / D half-whole** on the first pair, **G Dorian / C altered** on the second.

### Practice strategy

1. **A sections:** sing the melody's half-step motion (`G → Gb → F` in the bass line of bars 5–7) while comping. The whole A section is a slow chromatic descent.
2. **Bridge:** practice each pair of chords as its own key area (Gb major, B/F# ii–V, D7→Gm, Eb7, then ii–Vs home). Arpeggiate 1-3-5-7 across the bridge to lock in the root motion `Gb → B → F# → D → G → Eb → A → D → G → C`.
3. **Guide-tone line:** play only 3rds and 7ths through the bridge. They move by half or whole steps even though the roots jump.
