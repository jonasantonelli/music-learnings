## Study Notes

**Bar 1 (FMaj7):** Home base — use the **F major scale**. Aim for the chord tones (F A C E) on strong beats; the 4th (Bb) is the avoid note over the maj7.

**Bar 2 (Eb7):** bVII7 — a classic backdoor-flavored move out of FMaj7. Use **Eb Lydian b7** (from Bb melodic minor) for the #11 color.

**Bar 3 (Am7):** Diatonic iii chord — just stay in the **F major scale** (A Phrygian from A's perspective). Don't reach for A Dorian here; the F# would clash with the key.

**Bar 4 (D7):** V/ii setting up Gm7. The original chart writes altered D7 voicings (b5b9 / #5#9) — use **D Mixolydian b9 b13** (5th mode of G harmonic minor / Phrygian dominant) to capture that tension and pre-hear the resolution to Gm7.

**Bars 5–6 (Gm7):** Diatonic ii in F. **G Dorian** works, but **be careful with the natural 6 (E)** — it's the Dorian-defining note and sticks out over Gm7 since it's so close to the b7 (F) and the 5 (D). Treat it as a passing/color tone rather than a target.

**Bars 7–8 (Bbm7 → Eb7):** **Backdoor ii-V to F.** Instead of Gm7-C7 resolving up a fourth to F, the tune borrows iv (Bbm7) and bVII7 (Eb7) and resolves up a half step. Both chords share **Eb Lydian b7** (Bb melodic minor) — one scale across the whole ii-V. The leading tone is `Db → C` (the 5 of F).

**Bars 9–12 (Am7 → Dm7 → Gm7 → C7):** Descending root motion in fourths — the long approach to the major ii-V-I. Gm7-C7 is the home ii-V; Am7-Dm7 just extends the cycle backward. Stay in **F major** through Am7 and Dm7, then Dorian/Mixolydian on Gm7-C7.

**Bars 13–14 (Em7b5 A7(b9) → Dm7 G7):** Chain of ii-Vs descending by fourths. Em7b5-A7b9 is a **minor ii-V to Dm** — both chords share **A Mixolydian b9 b13** (Phrygian dominant from D harmonic minor), so you can play one scale across the whole bar. Then Dorian/Mixolydian for Dm7-G7.

**Bars 15–16 (Gm7 C7):** The final ii-V before resolving home (or back to top). Standard Dorian/Mixolydian.

**Bar 27 (Bm7b5):** New chord in the C section — voice-leads chromatically down toward Am7 via Bb7. Use **B Locrian**.

**Bar 28 (Bb7):** Chromatic approach — half-step descent from Bm7b5 down to Am7. Use **Bb Lydian b7** (which is F melodic minor — same notes, different root). The #11 (E) lands a half step above the root of Am7 for a smooth chromatic landing.

**Bars 29–30 (Am7 Dm7 | Gm7 C7):** Compressed version of the bar 9–12 cycle, two chords per bar. Same approach — F major through Am7/Dm7, Dorian/Mixolydian on Gm7/C7.

**Bar 31 (F6):** Final resolution — the tune lands on the major 6th instead of the maj7. Bar 32's `Gm7 C7` is the turnaround back to the top (omit on the final pass).
