## Study Notes

Standard Real Book changes; Joe Pass's solo guitar version on *Virtuoso* (1973) follows this harmony with his own passing chords.

The tune is a tour of key centers — **Ab → C → Eb → G → E → Ab** — each reached by diatonic cycle-of-fourths motion or a ii–V. Most of the melody is a chord tone held while the harmony moves under it, so it's a great tune for hearing *how the same note changes function*.

### A1 — Ab major, then C major

**Bars 1–4 (Fm7 / Bbm7 / Eb7 / AbMaj7):** vi–ii–V–I in Ab, one chord per bar. **Ab major** over all four; guide tones `Ab → Ab → G → G` (3rds and 7ths descend by half step).

**Bar 5 (DbMaj7):** IV of Ab — play **Db Lydian** (G natural) to smooth the move toward C.

**Bars 6–8 (Dm7 G7 / CMaj7):** ii–V–I to **C major**, a major third above Ab. The melody's `G` on DbMaj7 (the #11) becomes the 5 of C — the pivot note.

### A2 — same pattern a fourth higher

**Bars 9–12 (Cm7 / Fm7 / Bb7 / EbMaj7):** vi–ii–V–I in **Eb**. **Bar 13 (AbMaj7):** IV of Eb (**Ab Lydian**). **Bars 14–16 (Am7 D7 / GMaj7):** ii–V–I to **G major** — again a major third above the section's tonic.

### B — G major to E major

**Bars 17–20 (Am7 / D7 / GMaj7):** ii–V–I in G. **G major** throughout.

**Bars 21–23 (F#m7b5 / B7 / EMaj7):** ii–V–I to **E major**, a major third below G. The half-diminished ii is borrowed from minor — use **F# Locrian** (or E major from F# with a natural C), and **B Mixolydian b9 b13** / **B altered** on B7.

**Bar 24 (C7alt):** V of Fm7 — the enharmonic pivot: the melody's `G#` on EMaj7 becomes `Ab` (the b13 of C7). **C altered** or **C half-whole diminished**.

### A' — 12 bars, back to Ab with a longer ending

**Bars 25–29:** Same as A1 through DbMaj7.

**Bar 30 (Dbm7):** Minor iv — the **minor-plagal** color. **Db Dorian** or **Db melodic minor**; target `Fb (E)` falling to `Eb`.

**Bars 31–32 (Cm7 / Bdim7):** iii then a passing diminished sliding chromatically into Bbm7 — `Cm7 → Bdim7 → Bbm7` is a descending bass line. On Bdim7, **B whole-half diminished**.

**Bars 33–35 (Bbm7 / Eb7 / AbMaj7):** Final ii–V–I in Ab.

**Bar 36 (Gm7b5 C7(b9)):** Turnaround to Fm7 at the top — minor ii–V to the vi chord. **G Locrian**, then **C half-whole diminished**.

### Practice strategy

1. **Map the key centers** before playing a note: Ab, C, Eb, G, E, Ab. Play just the tonic triads in order.
2. **Guide tones through the cycle:** 3rds and 7ths move by half step or stay put through every vi–ii–V–I — play them as whole notes.
3. **Hear the pivot notes:** the melody's `G` (bar 5 → 6), `D` (bar 13 → 14) and `G#/Ab` (bar 23 → 24) connect each key change — sing them against the chords.
4. **Minor-plagal close:** loop bars 29–31 (`DbMaj7 → Dbm7 → Cm7`) until the Fb → Eb motion feels natural.
